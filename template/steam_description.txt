This mod is simply a rework of the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1983504552]original mod[/url].
Although NOT A SINGLE LINE of code was taken from the original, and everything was developed from scratch, credit for the idea still goes to the original author - [url=https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198025154321]@Stephen[/url]

Originally, I made this mod just for myself, but it turned out pretty well, so I decided to publish it.

[h2]List of changes compared to the original mod:[/h2]
[list]
[*]For all objects (plants, entities (debris, items, creatures, geysers, etc.), buildings from ruins, and others), the material is now displayed in the title[/*]
[*]The actual mass of the object is displayed (farm tiles, airflow tiles, etc., are processed correctly)[/*]
[*]Temperature is displayed without abbreviations[/*]
[*]The actual thermal conductivity of the object is displayed (including for insulated and rubber tiles)[/*]
[*]The Specific Heat Capacity of the material is displayed[/*]
[*]As a result of the correct mass, both thermal mass and heat energy are calculated correctly[/*]
[*]Heat Energy, like temperature, is displayed without abbreviations[/*]
[*]Phase-change temperatures are displayed in two units simultaneously (in the selected units and in kelvins)[/*]
[*]The temperature of the contents of the bubbles is displayed[/*]
[*]The content temperature of the bubbles is displayed[/*]
[*]In addition, the material selection section has been formatted[/*]
[/list]
(I may have forgotten something)
